# Pitch — CampusEscrow

**One-liner:** Escrow that protects student freelancers from ghosting and clients from no-shows — on-chain, 0% fees.

**Problem:** Students lose money to non-paying clients; clients don't trust unknown students; platforms take 10–20%.

**Solution:** A smart contract holds the money. Rules, not people, decide who gets paid:
- Approve → instant payout
- Client silent after delivery → student auto-claims
- Student misses deadline → client refunds
- Disagreement → arbiter splits funds

**Why Monad:** fast, cheap transactions make small student gigs (a few dollars) economical.

**Market:** ~200M+ college students globally; India alone has a huge student gig economy (design, content, dev, tutoring).

**Traction path:** campus clubs & college fests → student freelancer communities → stablecoin payments + reputation.

**Business model:** optional 1% fee on release / premium arbiter service (currently 0% for the hackathon).
